Revelations Main Easter Egg Walkthrough

1. Start The Easter Egg

  • Shoot the 4 Gravestones in Spawn in order (2 Richtofen, 3 Dempsey, 1 Nikolai, 4 Takeo).
  • When hit correctly, Red Flames appear on the Tombstones.

2. Map Setup

  • Build the Guard of Fafnir (Shield).
  • Acquire Keeper Protector Parts.
  • Acquire Apothicon Servant & Lil' Arnies from the Mystery Box.
  • Complete all 4 Corruption Engines.
  • Open Pack-a-Punch inside the Apothicon Stomach.

3. Keeper Protector Step

  • Locate a Triangle Shaped Stone at a Jump Pad:
    • Shangri-La to Spawn
    • Kino to Verrückt
    • Verrückt to Mob of the Dead
    • Origins to Mob of the Dead
  • Build and call the Keeper Protector at a nearby Altar (Requires 5,000 Points).
  • Lead the Keeper to the stone and protect it during the ritual (Lasts ~3 minutes).
  • After completion, pick up the Audio Reel and place it on the Main Stage in Kino Der Toten.

4. Lil' Arnie Step

  • Throw Lil' Arnies into the 3 holes inside the Apothicon Stomach (3 times each = 9 total).
  • Must not miss! If missed, everyone must leave and re-enter the Apothicon Stomach.
  • After 3 are thrown, 3 Margwas will spawn. Kill them to repeat until all 9 are done.
  • Collect the Audio Reel from the bridge and place it on the box in Nacht Der Untoten.

5. Collecting Bones

  • Apothicon Servant must be upgraded (Estoom-Ooth).
  • Must have a Pack-A-Punched bullet weapon.
  • Shoot all 6 Bones across the map using the upgraded Apothicon Servant:
    • Shangri-La: Above the Stamin-Up area / temple ruins.
    • Origins: On top of the wall near Generator 3 footprint.
    • Verrückt: Second floor, on the wall near the jump pad.
    • Kino: On the upper chandelier above the stage.
    • Der Eisendrache: On the wall during the wall-run section.
    • Nacht: Second floor, on the fallen roof structure outside.
  • Bones will appear as a skeleton in Nacht der Untoten (upper level).
  • Shoot the skeleton with an Arnie, then shoot Sophia's body with the upgraded Apothicon Servant.

6. Laser Turret Step

  • Go to each Corruption Engine Turret and aim them at the floating sky crystals.
  • After all crystals have lasers connected to them, head to Nacht der Untoten.
  • A large Sophia Orb will spawn. Follow it to Kino and let it interact with the teleporter.
  • Step into the teleporter to enter Samantha's room and pick up the Kronorium on the bed.

7. Apothicon Eggs & Gateworms

  • Place the Kronorium on the podium in Kino Der Toten.
  • Locate an Apothicon Egg (1 spawns at a time across set map locations).
  • Place the egg in one of the 4 pools inside the Apothicon Stomach and kill 10 Zombies near it.
  • Pick up the hatched Gateworm and use its audio ping to locate its Rune of Creation in the ground.
  • Repeat for all 4 Eggs/Gateworms to collect all 4 Runes of Creation.

8. Kino Simon Says Puzzle

  • Go to the Kino Projector Room and stand on the teleporter circle.
  • Interact with the Kronorium to reveal a 4-symbol Apothicon sequence.
  • Repeat the sequence by interacting with the corresponding symbols on the wall in order.
  • Complete 4 total rounds (Failing kicks everyone out and resets the round sequence).
  • Grab the Summoning Key from the center platform.

9. Summoning Key & Map Targets

  • Throw the Summoning Key at 7 specific items across the map:
    • Shangri-La: The Blue/Green Crystal/Gem atop the main temple stairs. .
    • Origins: The Tombstone at the top near Mob Stairs.
    • Der Eisendrache: The Clock Face above the Laser Gun.
    • Verrückt: Fountain in the courtyard outside.
    • Mob of the Dead: Poster inside the cell block.
    • Kino: Chandelier above the stage.
    • Nacht: The Barrel behind the window at bottom of main stairs

10. Final Boss Fight (Shadowman)

  • Head to the Kino Stage Teleporter and step inside to warp to Agartha.
  • Interact with the Kronorium on the altar to start the fight.
  • Place the Summoning Key in one of the 4 corner altars and fill it with soul kills.
  • Throw the filled key at Sophia to fire her laser at the Shadowman.
  • Shoot the Shadowman until he is pushed back inside the Apothicon Mouth.
  • Interact with the Kronorium in front of the mouth to finish him!
